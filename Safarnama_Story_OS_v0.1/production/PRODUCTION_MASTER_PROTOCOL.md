# Production Master Protocol

## Purpose
Define the immutable production pipeline for every Safarnama episode.

## Pipeline
Story → Pocket FM Adaptation → AI Performance → QA → Production Master

## Non-negotiable rules
- Never change story facts, plot, character psychology, or timeline during adaptation.
- Rewrite only for audio performance, clarity, pacing, and narration.
- Audio first; novelistic description is not a goal.
- Emotion must be established before dialogue.
- One paragraph = one visual/audio beat.
- AI narration must never be required to guess emotion, speaker intent, or pronunciation.
- Preserve continuity with the Bible, trackers, and prior approved episodes.
- Silence and pauses are intentional production elements.

## Production Master requirements
Each master must contain:
1. Episode metadata and version.
2. Approved adaptation script.
3. Scene/beat boundaries.
4. Explicit SFX/music cues only where production requires them.
5. Dialogue with speaker labels.
6. Narration written for AI performance.
7. Cliffhanger/end beat.
8. QA status.
9. Continuity status.
10. Source/adaptation provenance.

## Approval states
DRAFT → ADAPTATION READY → AI READY → QA PASS → PRODUCTION MASTER

No episode may be marked PRODUCTION MASTER if story continuity, character consistency, timeline, emotion, AI narration, or cliffhanger QA is unresolved.
